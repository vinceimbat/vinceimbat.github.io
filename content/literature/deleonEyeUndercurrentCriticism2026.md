Instead of gatekeeping, we want porosity.

Smuggling literary pleasures into theory

When does being an insider become a burden or tricky?
- Theory is tactile. We feel it in our bodies.
- insider-outsider
- you can't be super close to someone
- you can't have just one vantage point
- practice: what is this constant practice of proximity? how far and how close am I?
