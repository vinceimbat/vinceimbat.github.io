# Literature notes

In fact, Master Torajiro Okada lived in the United States for over three years about 120 years ago; during that time, he attended Quaker gatherings and was deeply impressed by them. Records of his observations remain:  
  
"Quaker meditation is virtually the same as Seiza."  
"I have learned more from the Quakers than from Zen."  
"When adopting the Quaker meditation posture and focusing one's energy on the point just below the navel, the body may sometimes begin to vibrate (quake)."  
"Quakers have no ministers. There is no need for professional teachers like ministers. It is sufficient for seniors and juniors to gather, meditate, and converse, thereby awakening spiritual wisdom and power from within."  
"(Demonstrating quietly while raising his knees) At Quaker gatherings, people wait for divine revelation in this very posture. The meditation and silent contemplation continue until someone in the group feels moved to speak or pray. Sometimes, the gathering concludes with meditation alone."  
"Emerson is the most outstanding figure among the Quakers."
