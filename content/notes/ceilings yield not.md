Ceilings yield not  
to monsoons  
and the storms they bring  
on a single night.  
Ceilings yielded to mandibles  
biting wood a second  
in between churning  
a wave of termites    
reproducing slowly  
for years.

I am ashamed to say  
I was raised in a house of God  
only to be spewed  
an apostate of fields.

To admit to myself  
I must stand alone  
To admit to others  
I need their help  
but I'm unable to say so  
that the house in Sta. Maria  
has cone close but it has never been  
lost in my dreams  
and the termites—  
they come to seek revenge.
