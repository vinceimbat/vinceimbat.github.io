Kay Rem

Kung ang buhay ay siyang tula
tulad ng parati mong sambit,
maiksi ang 'yong tula.
Subalit dahil dito,
mas ramdam ko ang hiwaga,
ang pagkamangha,
na sa bilyon-bilyong taong
maari sanang nahagip
ng banayad mong pluma,
naging serif ako
ng lumulundag na titik
sa payapa mong tula.

---
For Rem

If life is the poem
as you've always said,
yours is a short one.
But because of this,
I am filled with wonder
and awe,
that among billions
your gentle plume
could've touched,
I became a serif
of a lurching letter
on your placid poem.
