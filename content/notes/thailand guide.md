### Grand Palace Guide

Buy tix.
Go to Wat Phra Kaew
- Enter main entrance at the southwest corner (32)
- Enter Phra Ubosot (1)
- Return to entrance
- Wander off to the upper terrace
- Exit at Si Rattana Satsada Gate (31)
Go around other buildings
Rest at the shop/cafe between (25) and (6)
Visit Museum of the Emerald Buddha Temple (6)
Visit Queen Sirikit Museum of Textiles (3)

References
- https://dancarndt.wordpress.com/2020/01/14/self-guided-walking-tour-of-the-grand-palace-bangkok-thailand/

### Wat Pho Guide

Enter through the Western Gate (21)
Main attraction: Viharn Phranorn (16)
- Buy a bown of obol for 20 THB\
Explore buildings outside
Go to the East, return to four stupas (13)
Visit Phra Ubosot (1)

For CR, use those near the massage pavilions (14)

References
- https://dancarndt.wordpress.com/2020/04/07/self-guided-walking-tour-of-wat-pho-bangkok-thailand/
