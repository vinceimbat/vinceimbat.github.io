title: Guidelines on what to add to your reading inbox
---

When deciding reading materials to add to my [[reading inbox]], I prioritize the following:

- materials that are related to my current goals or projects
- materials that are both interesting and dense with information relevant to my goals, projects, and interests
- materials that are time-tested (i.e., classics)
- materials that survey a wide field or several fields (e.g., E.O. Wilson’s *Consilience*)
- autobiographies or biographies that summarize entire lifetimes of lessons

Having said that, I still add materials to read just for fun as long as I [[decide whether to read for fun or for learning]] and read accordingly.
