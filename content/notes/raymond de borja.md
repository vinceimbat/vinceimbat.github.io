Author of [[deborjaFacture2024|Facture]], [[as well in our estrangement de borja|as well, in our estrangement]], and [[deborjaDustContactThat2026|The Dust of a Contact That Is Everywhere]].

Raymond writes primarily to understand. [[poetry|Poetry]], for him, is a way to understand something.

He write prose as if it was a poem. I don't know if I could do this but if I do, I need to have a strong why.

He is informed by his many readings on poetry.

This is [[lyric philosophy]]. But I don't know if it is the kind I want to write. I want to write something that nurtures me and others. That people return to like scripture. But maybe this is too grand an aspiration.
