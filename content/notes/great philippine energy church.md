A rosary contains annotated sins  
a dictionary of prohibitions  
thou shall not be a writer  
if you are sinful and  
erudite at the same time.  
I searched for the synonym  
of chastity and beside  
blameless was beauty a  
compromise of unintelligible  
thesauruses rhinoceroses ferocious  
as they can be difficult to surmise.  
Peanut butter melts inside my stomach  
admittedly less potent than  
what Krip Yuson could stomach.
