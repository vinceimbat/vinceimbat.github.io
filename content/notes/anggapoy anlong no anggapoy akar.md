say akar  
so anlong  
say anlong  
so akar  

say madibinon  
kurang ed pikewet  
a dalan
so anlong

dakat irayan salita  
dapan so pankurit  
mantutumbuka'y kipelag  
na sarayan talinta-o

ed pitek  
na ilalam  
ed pilapil  
na nonot tan pakatebek

mantaltaliwa'k  
ed abetay tawen  
dalin  
siak sika

sikami sikatayo  
sikara  
sikara  

say talba  
so anlong  
ed akar  
na talba
