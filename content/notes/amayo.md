Ombëbëtël a amin so nadiwit  
na Amian.

Layag, bëklëw, limak  
manaalmoy kulibënbën.

Pansisinggawa'y liwawa tan bilungët  
irayan mantikyab a kamanokan.

Onlëlërëg mët so banwa sanën
lakap-lakap ta ka'd saman a ngarëm. 

Ambëtël mët so dagëm balët  
maëkëm ëd bëklëw mo.

Tan saman a bëkta ët singa mët  
sarayan kamanokan.

Tinmitikiyab la 'ran bulos
ëd arapën a ag mo ak lan balot kaiba.

***

Patalos ëd Ingles

Endearment

The Amihan chills
everything it touches.

My ears, my neck, my hands
seek for a wrap.

Light and darkness jostle
for these birds in flight.

The sun was also setting
that afternoon I embraced you.

The wind was also cold
but your neck was warm.

And that moment
was like these birds.

Freed, flying into a future
without me by your side.
