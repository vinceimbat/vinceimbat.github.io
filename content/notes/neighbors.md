I was never blessed with good neighbors  
and so when the wire that connected our  
apartment complex's electrical system  
to the post snapped yielding  
to the weight of that young wild tree  
leaving us with no light for more  
days after light has returned to the  
entire street I knew I must go out  
and knock at the three doors next to us:  
the door of mormon missionaries,  
children sent to the Philippines to be elders,  
next to the door of a young couple and their two children,  
and the door of an old couple and their dog.

I stood there with the two men  
staring at the post  
plotting a strategy to fix it  
volunteering action in between  
volunteering stories from our pasts  
about how her mother lives alone  
in Pampanga and about how his  
children in Canada will take them  
there soon.

I walked back to our door that night  
inside a house still dark  
lit only by two candles  
but in my chest I felt  
a light snap  
a light whose brightness will fill  
this house and beyond.
