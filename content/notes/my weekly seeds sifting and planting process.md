Every day, I accumulate seeds in the daily notes of my [[talahardin]], in the Drafts app, and in my journal. A habit of returning to these seeds and processing them to identify various writing species I could extract from them and plant into a separate [[notes/seedlings|seedlings]] note is essential. Although I do return to seeds in my journal when I index them during [[my daily routine]], this isn't enough. The seeds that could be planted into seedlings must be sifted and planted

Currently, I've scheduled this process during Mondays, right after [[sulat nilay|Sulat Nilay]].

It is sifting through materials to see whether there is a poem, essay, vignette, or a note there.
