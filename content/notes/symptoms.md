Created: 17 June 2015

Life  
Then darkness  
Life  
Then darkness  
Then day  
Then night

Frightened  
Hopeless  
Sleepless  
Screams but no one hears  
Cries but tears don’t fall  

Then  
Death comes  
Knocking gently at his door  
Walking quietly to his room  

Dressed in a silvery evening gown  
White as pearl  
Attractive  
Provocative  
Seductive  
She looks at his eye  
And smirks  

He calls Death,  
“Come, lay down beside me."  
He pulls the ribbon from her hair.  
Holds her arms  
And pull her near his face

Death...  
Death...  
Oh, lovely Death...  
Sweet, alluring, enticing Death...

No one makes love like you do  
No one

Let me rest  
For death in peace is more beautiful  
Than a life of pain and suffering
