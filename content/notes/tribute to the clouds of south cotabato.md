title: Tribute to the Clouds of South Cotabato
---

Banquets and banquets where life all starts  
Myriads and myriads of fearless clouds  
Wings of which fly on deafening winds  
Souls who stay unrestlessly

Once nonetheless the creeps are true  
Voices unheard bother nights dew  
Thousands of beads-men dancing with death  
Dark in the hallows of hell's feet bed  

A heart I learned to throw off ground  
And picked before I left the mound  
Surpassing the TEARS  
the RIVERS  
the FALLS  
And finally worth dying on ends of its calls.

I thought I was already a Filipino  
'Til the crescent and cross said I was not  
That way I saw the difference  
of walking with streetlights and streets alone.

I'm coming home with tearless eyes  
Bade from a mother's bossom and breathes deep cry

I'd say I rather kill a fellowman  
Than bring my quail on neverland
