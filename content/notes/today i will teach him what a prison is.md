title: Today I Will Teach Him What a Prison Is
---

I told him to walk  
beside the river  
and gather things  
that were long.

An hour is a breeze  
when he returned  
on his arms  
a dried vine,  
a timeworn halyard,  
a fishing line,  
and wrapped  
around his head  
a torn bandana.

Seeing my mistake,  
I took a chance  
and asked him  
if he could cage  
anything  
with his things.

He laid them  
on the ground  
he laid them  
one after the other  
in a circle  
around him and I

around tears  
I found later  
dripping  
from my eyes  
to my knees  
kneeling  
on mud

his hand  
over mine.
