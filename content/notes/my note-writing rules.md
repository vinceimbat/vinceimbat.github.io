title: My note-writing rules
---

1. No daily processing of notes.
2. No processing of old notes in inbox.
3. Only process notes when they resurface naturally.
4. Stop publishing notes (keep current notes as an archive).
5. Be strictly minimal about creating new notes. Only write notes when absolutely necessary. Only write about my own ideas or how my ideas develop alongside others. (I noticed that the notes that are really useful to me and that I often return to are those that maintain my system as a whole. The others could be organized as messy notes in the journal instead.)
6. Use project notes as scratch paper to link to other notes, including reading annotations, prompts, and journal entries.
