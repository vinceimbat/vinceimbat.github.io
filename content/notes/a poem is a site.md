A letter may be a site. I am sure a word is a site. A phrase is a site. A sentence is a site.

When placed alongside each other, sites create a field.

When placed alongside each other, species of language create a field. The writer creates a field. 

Now things become even more interesting when the words, phrases, and sentences in a text (say a poem) refer to existing site.

To do: Write a poem that reconstructs a physical field into something new.
