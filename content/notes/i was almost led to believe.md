a flower will not grow here  
but the books came  
and with them tales of a foreign land  
and suddenly you found yourself  
floating on flooded ground  
from the vast ocean underneath  
wet soil into loam  
and the tales  
cracked your shell  
hardened by numbers  
and insistence for a better life  
and from you sprouted  
a bud of afternoon grass  
with you the hero  
in monologue with the monster  
yourself  
you are stronger than you think  
and they are frightened of you  
yourself, dwarves, and giant in one large  
unbounded universe.
