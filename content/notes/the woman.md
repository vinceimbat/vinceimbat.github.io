Created: 3 May 2015

The woman  
Is lying down in front of me  
In my bed  
Half-naked  
Her shirt off  
She sleeps soundly  
As I type these words  
On my MacBook

Her hands folded above her head  
Her legs crossed  
Her breasts alluring  
Her button expanding  
Then shrinking  
Then expanding

Momentarily  
She raises her hand  
To scratch her head  
But she does so  
Unconsciously  
Unknowingly  
That I’m watching  
Smiling

As she rests her hands  
Upon her breasts  
Concealing  
What was once exposed

She breathes the air that I breathe  
She dreams the dreams that I dream
