# Rizal's religion

Here are some readings to explore this further:
- Bonoan, Raul J., S.J. 1987. Religion and nationalism in Rizal. In The Noli me tangere—a century after: An interdisciplinary perspective. Edited by Soledad S. Reyes. Budhi Papers No. 7. Quezon City: Phoenix Publishing House, Inc.
- Bonoan, Raul J., S.J. 1992. The enlightenment, deism, and Rizal. Philippine Studies 40.
- Bonoan, Raul J., S.J. 1994. The Rizal-Pastells correspondence. Quezon City: Ateneo de Manila University Press.
- Guerrero, Leon Ma. 1974. The first Filipino. Manila: National Historical Commission.
- Pascual, Ricardo A. 1962. The philosophy of Rizal. Manila: Pedro B. Ayuda and Company.
- Rizal, Jose and Pablo Pastells. 1961. Dr. Jose Rizal-Fr. Pablo Pastells religious controversy. Quezon City: R. Martinez and Sons.
- Schumacher, John N., S.J. 1965. The religious thought of Rizal. Philippine Studies 13.
