"From the root the sap rises up into the artist, flows through him, flows to his eyes. He is the trunk of the tree. Overwhelmed and activated by the force of the current, he conveys his vision into his work....he does nothing other than gather and pass on what rises from the depths. He neither serves nor commands, he transmits. His position is humble. And the beauty at the crown is not his own: it has merely passed through him." — Paul Klee
