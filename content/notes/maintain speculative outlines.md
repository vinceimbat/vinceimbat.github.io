Whenever I write in the [[talahardin]], whether that's writing [[notes/seedlings]] or developing [[notes/evergreens]], I try to connect these new notes to existing speculative [[notes/outline|outlines]] tracked in an [[index of speculative outlines]]. If an outline about a topic I'm writing about doesn't exist, I create one. I could easily create an outline by comparing two notes and asking myself which should come first. The key to creating this outlines is to always think of them as tentative until they reach a level of finality fit for sharing or publication. This approach is an integral part of [[my writing process]] and helps me execute my [[talahardin writing strategy]] by ensuring that I have enough outlines to turn into writing projects.
