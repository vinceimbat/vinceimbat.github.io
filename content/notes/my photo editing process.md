# Culling

- Create a collection set for the project.
- Under the collection set, create a collection called Cull 1. For this first cull, be liberal in sending any photo that is mildly interesting into the Cull 1 collection.
- Create a new collection called Cull 2. Go through Cull 1 and go through the details of each photograph (e.g., light, patterns, and how they fit into the story you are creating). Basically, identify photos worth spending more time at.

# Editing

- Go through at least two steps of edits, which you should do on an iPad.


***

Try to edit without using presets.

Reduce noise by lessening luminance and adding clarity.
