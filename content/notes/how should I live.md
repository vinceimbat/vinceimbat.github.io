This is an "ethical" question (i.e., a question about how one ought to act based on justifications that represent one's beliefs on what is right or wrong). It is different from the question [[how to live|"How to live?"]]. It presupposes that there is a certain way that one “must” live.

When you ask this question, you are interested about uncovering or even building a process, system, or practice that you prescribe to yourself. This system inspires [[action]] and provides justification for them.

The first step in answering this question is to know what you are looking for. To find this, you need to find a way to know what you are looking for ([[philosophy]]). Discovering what you are looking for involves asking yourself how you know what you know ([[epistemology]]) and what exists ([[metaphysics]]).
