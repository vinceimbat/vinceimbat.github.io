Why pursue experimental writing when I want to use writing as a religious discipline?

Experimental writing leads to epiphany or self-reflection in a facilitative way.

To keep my secret unearthed, I typed it on a piece of paper, cut each letter and, from my house, walked to separate points hiding each letter under a piece of rock in the middle of a field or under a house. I called the piece "My Secret" and I'm selling it to you for Php 50. I require trust for this transaction to push through.
