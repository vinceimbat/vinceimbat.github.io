Every day, I follow [[my daily routine]]. The events below break the routine and are inserted into the specific time indicated.

# Monday

## Morning

- Read and study Andrew James Brown's latest sermon or a previous one.

## Afternoon

- [[my weekly review routine|My weekly review routine]]

## Evening

- Reconcile funds.

# Tuesday

## Morning

- Perform a gathering walk.
- Write a Substack newsletter draft based on the gathering walk.

# Wednesday

## Morning

- 8:00–9:00 AM: Attend Japanese seiza.
- Continue with other writing projects (esp. for website essays, zines, books, or submissions).

# Thursday

## Morning

- Write a poem in response to this week's Mountain Beacon prompt.
- Continue with other writing projects (esp. for website essays, zines, books, or submissions).

## Evening

- (Fortnightly) Attend Kiitsu Kyōkai.

# Friday

## Morning

- Continue with other writing projects (esp. for website essays, zines, books, or submissions).

## Evening

- 7:00–9:00 PM: Attend Poetry Fridays.

# Saturday

## Morning

- Revise and schedule Substack newsletter.
- Continue with other writing projects (esp. for website essays, zines, books, or submissions).

## Evening

- (As scheduled) 8:00 PM: Attend English seiza.

# Sunday

## Morning

- Revise and send a poem to Mountain Beacon.

## Afternoon

- Prepare for and attend Nilay.

## Evening

- (Monthly or as scheduled) 7:00 PM: Attend Jōdo Shinshū.
