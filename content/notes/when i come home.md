The smell of raw oysters  
unsevered from their shells  
announce my arrival in Binmaley.

On my kitchen shelf in Laguna salt  
has overflown from a bottle  
of bagoong months after  
it has remained unopened.

Bathe me with smoke  
from burning goat skin  
the stench of chewed grass  
on cow dung beside hay in March  
so that I may remember.

Along Mangaldan plaza  
I walk beside women darkened  
by the sun fan on coal  
weakened kindle burning banana
leaves wrapped around sweetened
rice and coconut.
