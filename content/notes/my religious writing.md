I characterize the writing I do as [[religious writing]] contending with the idea of [[jiyu shukyo|jiyū shūkyō]].

The primary function of my writing is to support my religious life. Because of this, the primary trajectory of my writing is inward (i.e., [[the writing i do in private is my most important writing|the writing I do in private is my most important writing]]). That said, I also want my writing to connect me with my [[kapwa]] (and through that connection to the cosmos). This is the secondary function of my writing life.

Since my thoughts about my [[writing]] changes a lot these days as I still am developing [[my poetics]], I'm tracking [[my current thoughts about my writing]] on a log of that name.

What is the aim?
How am I achieving this aim?

# Styles

I'm currently studying and experimenting with the following styles of writing associated with religious writing. I'm thinking about these influences in the context of [[poetry]], but I imagine it will influence my prose as well. My goal is to decide whether to adopt one or all as styles in my religious writing. These three styles are as follows:

1. Traditional religious writing exemplified by the works of [[denise levertov|Denise Levertov]] and [[thomas merton|Thomas Merton]].
2. Down-to-earth writing of the [[bagay|Bagay]] exemplified by the works of [[rofel brion|Rofel Brion]].
3. Flow writing characteristic of the [[beat poets|Beat poets]] exemplified by the works of [[allen ginsberg|Allen Ginsberg]] (and perhaps, Clark Coolidge).
4. Language writing exemplified by the works of [[norman fischer|Norman Fischer]] and his influences.
5. Classical Japanese writing exemplified by the works of [[basho|Bashō]].
