title: McDonald's
---

After shouting at the ground personnel  
he returned to his seat—fuck!  
waiting for him some McDonald's lunch  
inside a styro and a drink.

He turned to the body on the other  
bench and asked her,  
"Aren't you incensed? They are  
making us wait for uncertainty!"  
All he got was a weird nod.

In my country, the day we are  
born is the day we start our  
lifelong training of patience.  
We've accepted scarcity, we've accepted  
centuries of oppression. There is  
no use for speaking up. Our  
silence, inaction,—either a precious  
inheritance or a terrible  
control mechanism.

An alternative: Acceptance are  
revolt—"Nothing can ever disturb  
me. I choose peace."
