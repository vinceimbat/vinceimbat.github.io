People think we are weak  
To have given up in that moment  
We wanted to end our own suffering.  
Everything is a competition  
Even staying alive for a moment  
Is a competition.  
These words aim for intelligibility  
For you to understand how hard it was  
To come from that  
And to choose to stay  
And I ought to paint this thing  
With sophisticated metaphors  
To hide what I want to say  
Or to convince you to stay here  
To listen to me  
To save me from myself  
But I do not want to burden myself of that  
Anymore  
I have been burdened  
By illnesses and medications

These words are enough  
Proof that I've chosen to stay  
I have stayed.  
I will stay.

Will you stay  
With me?
