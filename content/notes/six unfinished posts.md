title: Six Unfinished Posts

About 50 steps from me  

six unfinished posts  

protruding out of concrete  
rust  
a rusty roof  
planks  
waiting  
to fall down  
and leaves  
lots of leaves  
covering  
hiding decay  

the sun rises  
and sets  
above this place  
everyday  
and with it  
the rust grows  
iron  
concrete  
e x p a n d s  

the leaves  
they dry  
they fall  

they fall.
