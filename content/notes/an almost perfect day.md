This is an almost perfect day.  
No putrid smoke.  
Just fresh clean air.  
No disturbing noise.  
Just delicious silence.
A little heat in the morning.  
And cold in the evening and late afternoon.  
Beautiful.  
Ordinary days.  
Parts and parcels of life.  
Life that is rather meaningless.
