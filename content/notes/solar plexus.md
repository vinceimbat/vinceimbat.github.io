title: Solar Plexus

My seiza sensei said one must sit  
on the front third of the chair  
to breathe correctly. One must  
straighten one's back he said
  
with the solar plexus slightly bent  
forward as the entire torso is  
bent forward. Imagine a mountain  
he said. With your temple
  
the summit. Remember to pull  
your legs backward motioning  
with his hands. And then with  
hands clamped together on the
  
corner of your lap and belly he said 
begin to exhale slowly slowly slowly.  
One row of the jeep fits only nine  
but the driver insisted we make room
  
for one more. I moved my butt  
on the front fifth of my seat  
the jeep's ceiling forcing me to  
bend forward with an arc on my
  
solar plexus I pull my legs  
backward and began gasping  
for air as the passenger beside me  
holds on the bar and we began moving.
