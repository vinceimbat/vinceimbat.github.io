My late best friend. The Batangueño poet who translated the [[tzuTaoTeChing2006|Tao te Ching]] to Filipino. He established the [[tungko ng tula|Tungko ng Tula]], a group for those starting into [[poetry]] in 2020 at the beginning of the COVID-19 pandemic.
