Per [[williams carlos williams|William Carlos Williams]], a poetry is a field of action, which [[charles olson|Charles Olson]] adopted.

As a writer, one must see the page as a field, a space to fill up. As for me, I see it as a field of spiritual and religious practice that is both collective and personal. It is a field for the expansive self.
