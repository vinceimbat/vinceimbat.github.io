v2

She listened to me at that hour.  
Witnessed my flickering light fade.  
Her sister sleeping as I carried her  
                        ]head over  
        ]shoulder as we walked

                        ]the Old Testament  
            ]document of Israelite fury  
An Exodus before Genesis  
                        ]a myth for  
            ]-building

         ]that[                      ]burns
              ]and I say with vigor
to consume every line of skin  
  ]shut the door on a vulgar's mouth  
            escape before the temple falls.

Stripped[                ], I  
     ]take the pulpit. Stripped  
   ]God[       ]write my  
scripture. Stripped of the  
            church I say Amen.
