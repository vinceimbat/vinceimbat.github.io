There are three parts to my [[annual review]] process:

1. Aggregate the annual report.
2. Perform the core of the annual review.
3. Write the annual review article and publish it (Optional).

# Part I: Aggregate the annual report

1. Start the process on January 3 of the year (this is to give way for [[my monthly financial review process]] and [[monthly review]] first).
2. Copy and paste the summary of each [[monthly reports|monthly report]] into a new [[annual reports|annual report]] using the [[annual report|annual report template]].
3. Use the annual report to accomplish the core of the annual review.

# Part II: Perform the core of the annual review

1. [[gratitude lists|Gratitude list]]: Write a gratitude list.
2. [[notes/past and coming year questions|Past and coming year questions]]: Review my past year and think about the present year.
3. [[my life areas|My life areas]]: Review areas of responsibility and set new standards if needed.
4. [[life goals|Life goals]]: Add new goals and corresponding projects in [[my life goals master list]]. Refer to [[how to set goals]].
4. [[personal narrative vision|Personal narrative vision]]: Review last year’s personal narrative vision and write next year’s.
5. Routines and reviews: Review last year’s [[routines]] and update them if necessary.
6. Review [[my workview]] and update if necessary.
7. Review [[my lifeview]] and update if necessary.
8. Share the results of my annual review to at least one person close and important to me.

# Part III: Write the annual review article and publish it (Optional)

1. Write a draft of the previous year’s [[annual review article]].
2. Edit.
3. Format as a blog post, publish, then share in social media.
4. Format it as a newsletter article then send it to my email list.
