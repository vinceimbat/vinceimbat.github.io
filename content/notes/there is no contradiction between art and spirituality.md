There has to be no contradiction between [[art]] and [[spirituality]]. I'm not taking away time from building or nurturing a spiritual life when I do art. This isn't the case and shouldn't be the case. It only feels this way when my art practice is muddled by the chasing of outward achievements: awards, fellowships, publications, and publicity. It doesn't feel this way when I focus on [[journaling]], [[talahardin]], my own [[notes/walking]] projects, my own [[writing]] projects, and nurturing [[nilay|Nilay]] and my other communities of writers.
