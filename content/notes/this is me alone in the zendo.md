title: This is Me Alone in the Zendo
---

This is me  
Alone in the zendo  
Tired  
Of the outside world

This is me  
Sitting tight on a chair  
Peaceful  
Like lonely flowers

This is me  
But I can't see myself  
Wicked  
Like dull magic tricks

This is me  
Alone in the zendo  
Waiting
