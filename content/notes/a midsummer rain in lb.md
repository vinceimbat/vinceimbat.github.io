title: A Midsummer Rain in LB
---

When water dies  
of terrible heat  
in gray clouds  
it rains in LB.

The dead pour  
in brisk drizzles  
and the wind  
whispers a prayer.

Touching mud  
their souls flutter  
as brown bugs into  
the last string of sunlight.
