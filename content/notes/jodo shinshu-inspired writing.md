A [[jodo shinshu|Jōdo Shinshū]] way of [[writing]] will perhaps be one that doesn't rely too much on will and skill. One that relies on the timing of the natura world. Practice is probably questionable. Practice is more [[zen|Zen]] thinking. So, writing inspired by Jōdo Shinshū should probably produce less. Its forms might be rough. Draft-like. Unfinished. It will stop whenever flow stops.
