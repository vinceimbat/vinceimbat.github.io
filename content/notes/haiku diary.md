a student overtakes

[[2025-11-06]]

Yellow slipper
Behind yellow slipper
Yellow light

Some green leaves dancing
after being touched by sun
as clouds hold their rain

Many days of rain
Under this thick canopy
Moss on edges of the pathway

Soft green moss

Many days of rain
Thick soft moss on the pathway
I walk more slowly

Sun and rain blessed them
Bushy vines on wires hanging
Stretched until when?

Cold begins to descend
Towering trees
Child looks out the window

Tayaketek la
ason nimani'd keltang
batik ed sirom

Moth flew on the middle of the road
Hides behind a leaf
I fear
