I use the word poetics liberally to refer to my personal beliefs on what art is and how I want to practice it. It also brushes into how I understand and approach creativity. [[thoughts on my poetics|My poetics]] is simply an articulation of a framework that I try to follow when I create. I try to articulate it to express the intricate connection between the act of creative production and the life I seek to live.
