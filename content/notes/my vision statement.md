I have brought to life [[my jiyu shukyo|my jiyū shūkyō]]—a free, creative, inquiring, and liberative religion and spirituality—customized to my needs, personality, strengths, talents, and interests. It is a [[jiyu shukyo|jiyū shūkyō]] that is also anchored to my culture. This way of living informs how I exist in the world—[[my daily routine|my daily practices]], the work that I do, and how I [[action|act]]. While it liberates me, it also strengthens my faith and connection with my *kapwa* both the human and the more-than-human. I'm part of a [[kiitsu kyokai|community]] of individuals walking on the same path who support me in the realization of my vision.
