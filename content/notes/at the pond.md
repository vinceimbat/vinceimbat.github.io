Bird shrieking  
Cows munching  
Three kingfishers  

diving...  

   diving...  

      diving…

Invisible insects  
making  

ripples…  

   ripples…  

      ripples…

A heron—large and white—  
passes…  

Sunlight on my face  
on the pages of this notebook  
creating shadows on the water.
