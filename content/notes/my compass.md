While destinations in life isn't always clear, one can be clear in one's direction. To achieve this measure of clarity, a life compass could be useful.

My compass is made up of:
- [[my purpose statement]]
- [[my vision statement]]
- [[my mission statement]]
- [[my lifeview]]
- [[my workview]]

I review my compass regularly, which I do during [[my monthly review routine]]. I also use it whenever I feel off course in life or are going through a major transition. When this happens, I recalibrate my compass. I also recalibrate my compass once a year during [[my annual review routine]].
