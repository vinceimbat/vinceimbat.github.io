title: Poetry techniques

- Combine thoughts with images.
- Repeating first word/ rhyme at the beginning.
- Just put images after each other with no real reason.
- Address it to a specific person (e.g., lola)
- Italicizing lines to invite reinterpretation
- Separate one thought into two stanzas.
- Use title as the first line of the poem.
- Sir D: multiple images from one situation juxtaposed to each other with no clear connections
- Images created by larger chunks of text (i.e., more lines)
- Write a poem of images from an entire day.
- Use parentheses.
- Use indents.
