title: Writing makes spirituality less intellectual

[[writing|Writing]], meaning creative as well as uncreative writing, is helping me be less intellectual in my spirituality. As I focus on short forms ([[notes/vignette]], [[notes/poems]], prose poems, etc.), I give space to silence while still welcoming words. But the keyword here is "focus." This doesn't mean that I don't have and will not produce works like Andrew's. Opening myself to all possibilities and growth is important to me.
