Seeds are random ephemeral thoughts that come to me during walks or throughout the day. Seeds are kept as is based on the suggestion of [[henry david thoreau|Henry David Thoreau]] and [[henry bugbee|Henry Bugbee]] that raw insights have to be cherished and their rawness preserved.

Perhaps seeds should be captured through a small analog notebook because some insights need to be drawn.
