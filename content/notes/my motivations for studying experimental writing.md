I ought to ask myself then: what is my motivation for exploring experimental writing? The clearest response and alignment is that I'm also a meditator. I do [[seiza]], which is still connected with Zen. But how does [[jiyu shukyo|jiyū shūkyō]] and [[jodo shinshu|Jōdo Shinshū]] align here?

- By removing the [[self]] in the page, I allow *tariki* to take over—bahala na. I let go of the tyranny of coherence, logic, beauty, and linearity. A *tariki*-inspired writing puts less emphasis on craft (or at least the kind of effortful craft that overly relies on a writer's talent).
- Language poetry also is tightly connected with issues of power and economy. This is where *jiyū shūkyō* enters with its strong emphasis on the larger whole.

The challenge for myself is to still create scripture, a balm to myself and others even if I choose to produce experimental writing.
