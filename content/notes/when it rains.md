title: When It Rains
---

Rain reminds us  
Of how fragile we are,  
Of how much we want to be alive.  
We thirst for life.  
Rain reminds us that we want health  
More than anything else in life  
Rain reminds us that there is a world out there  
Bigger than our laptops and smartphones  
Mountains, oh blue mountains!  
They too come alive when it rains.  
For all of us are astonished  
When nature heeds our call  
And rain pours heavily on a barren tired land  
Because we all are broken  
Or rather, we “feel” broken,  
Even if we’re not  
And we need a gentle, soft caress  
Of a summer afternoon shower.
