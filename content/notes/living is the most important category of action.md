[[living|Living]] is the most important [[action]] among the [[five areas of my life work]]. It is the most important part of what I have to do overall. The quality of the other categories of action and my creative work in general is affected by the quality of how I live.

Living is the ultimate battleground where everything I learn from [[understanding]] converge. I do the other categories of action to live a good life in the first place. Therefore, it is necessary to have a [[hierarchy of categories of actions in my work]].

Thinking about, optimizing, and acting to improve [[my life areas]] of my life is part of the work, if not the most important work. Also, [[living can be part of the creative work]]. [[the art of living is my gift]].
