title: Things Are Clearer Now
---

I could start  
Dreaming  
Again.

There is no  
Pressing need  
To know further  
Than I already know

I am convinced  
Of the more basic truths  
I am comfortable  
Believing on assumptions

And starting on an adventure  
To, indeed, know how to live
