# Writing

## How long have you been writing regularly?

I started writing regularly since I started my journaling practice around 2018. So that's about seven years already.

## How much do you write each week?

Now, I write at least one to three pages in my journal daily (around 500 words daily). I take notes while outdoors (around 100 words daily). I also write notes in my [[talahardin]] (around 500 words daily). On top of these, I publish one newsletter each month (1,000 words). I write probably 7,000–8,000 words per week.

## Is your schedule structured or sporadic?

More structured than sporadic. The only time I break my schedule is when I work for seven to ten days each month, I travel, or when I have an important event to attend. That said, I write whenever insight or motivation arrives, so this mode is sporadic.

## Are you focusing on one project or experimenting with various ideas?

Right now, I am experimenting with multiple ideas.

# Reading

## Do you read regularly? How many books per year?

Yes. Last year (2024), I read 23 books. Since 2015, I average 10 books a year. However, I need to be more regular, systematic, and intentional with my reading.

## What are your three favorite topics or genres?

I like reading lyrical creative nonfiction books that delve into philosophical, religious, or theological matters. I also like biographical books that tell the story of someone worth emulating. I like books that experiment with walking or that are situated in a specific time and space (historical and geographical). Lastly, I like poetry books of all kinds—formalist, conceptual, or experimental.

## What are the last five books you read?

1. [[first we read then we write richardson|First We Read, Then We Write by Robert D. Richardson Jr.]]
2. Deaf Republic by Ilya Kaminsky
3. [[will you tell me what i look like coronel|will you tell me what I look like? by Raphael Atienza Coronel]]
4. [[deborjaFacture2024|facture by Raymond de Borja]]
5. [[this is how to mean no harm villanueva|This is How to Mean No Harm by Martin Villanueva]]

# Community

## Do you have writer friends or colleagues?

Yes. I have more serious writer friends now, and my work is influenced by theirs.

## Do you have a critique group where you can exchange feedback?

None. Although, I sometimes ask for help from my writer friends.

## Have you gone to any events or conferences?

Yes. But not often. I think I need to do a better job in identifying events and attending them.

## Do you read any writing blogs, magazines, or websites?

Not really on writing per se. But I do read blogs and websites of writers I admire or whose works influence mine.

## Do you connect with writers on social media? If so, where?

Yes. Facebook and IG.

# Miscellany

## What is the biggest "win" you've experienced in your writing, reading, or community building this past year?

Last year, my essay won in the GBL. I read a wide variety of material that went into my own work both in content and form. And my network has grown more. 

## If you could improve one thing in your writing life, what would that be?

Living it as a spiritual or religious disicipline.
