She was there  
Then she was gone  
She made my morning  
A beautiful one

The sky was blue  
The sun up high  
And perched on a branch  
Was the beautiful (...?)

It's been a struggle  
To wake up in the morning  
But when the bato-bato greets me  
With its royal blue feathers  
And hollow cry  
My body and mind (...?)  
I arise
