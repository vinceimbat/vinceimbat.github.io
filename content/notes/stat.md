I used to hate statistics  
in high school they made you  
calculate the sum of shit by hand  
formulas were all that matter  
Expensive calculators  
You had to learn them for grades  
But now I had to write them for work  
42 percent of excess heat goes to ocean  
Percent of have no clean water  
Percent of women

I write them statistics so much  
I started to care

There is a 1 percent chance  
You will ever read this  
(Obviously I made that up)

But come to think of it  
Statistics is what we do  
To people to help other people  
Comprehend how much they should care  
Otherwise,  
It's all formula  
Expensive calculators  
Highschool bullshit
