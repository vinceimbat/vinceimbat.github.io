title: Dust

Let the sweeping be for later, because in the heavens  
another star is collapsing,  
carried off by history, clinging to the air,  
unseen until it happens to align with  
a lone ray of sunlight
only acquires meaning when it joins the others,  
when it soils the house, makes the nose sneeze,  
a passage for the final breath before  
the fall of us all.
