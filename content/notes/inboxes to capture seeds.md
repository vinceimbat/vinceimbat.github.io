I capture [[notes/seeds]] into three inboxes:

1. Drafts app to capture seeds on the fly, particularly when walking.
2. A digital journal.

These inboxes are drained regularly in different schedules because [[efficient inboxes are reliably drained]]. Seeds in Drafts and my journal are reviewed to identify interesting writing prompts. These prompts are transferred to the [[talahardin]], with tags used to create a virtual [[writing inbox]] for these notes that will eventually be processed into [[notes/evergreens]].
