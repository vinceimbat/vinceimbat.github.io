What do you need glasses for  
if there's no blue sky?

Diwariwariwap  
aliw sa dog yapyap  
haba ng hairyup

But I have everything you need:  
brown glasses  
big glasses  
thick glasses  
tabingi on my round hamster cheeks

It's been raining for days  
sky blue erased  
habagat never fair  
thankfully—

I have kaway hair
