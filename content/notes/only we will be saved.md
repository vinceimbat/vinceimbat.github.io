This is how the Holy Spirit anoints—  
it chooses only a single group,  
sometimes a single nation or a single race,  
like a fine mist that pours down only here  
and never, ever pours down there.

Only men are crowned—  
they must be educated, must be minted,  
spotless, eloquent,  
—but above all, their bellies must be large.  
("This is my beloved Son, in whom  
I am well pleased.")

And when the tongues  
like fire begin to blaze, bow your head,  
kiss the muzzles of their guns,  
spread yourself open—and feel the heat  
of the slow, deliberate entry  
of this good news.
