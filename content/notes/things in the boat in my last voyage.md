A few scoops of warm rice and cuts of boiled meat. The banana leaf that wraps them. The oil to anoint. A few pieces of clothes to warm me in the middle of an unmoving river. A nugget to give the boatman when we reach the other shore. Strands of Mauricio's hair after years of waiting in the grave. And his browned flesh adhered to his bones

*the songs of sparrows*
*the comfort in the air*
*firefly stars*
