title: Metalearning

Metalearning means learning about learning. We perform metalearning to create a personalized plan for a [[self-directed learning]] project (see also [[ultralearning]]). Metalearning prevents us from getting lost or choosing a learning path unwisely. The more we practice metalearning, the better we do it in subsequent projects (i.e., in gathering resources, time management, managing motivation, and building confidence).

[[do metalearning by asking why what and how|Do metalearning by asking why, what, and how]]. For an example of how meta-learning was used in writing, see [[james clear used ultralearning to improve his writing|how James Clear used ultralearning to improve his writing]].
