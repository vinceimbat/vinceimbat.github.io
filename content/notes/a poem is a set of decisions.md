A poem is a canvas, a field. It is a set of decisions. You have to decide. And what are you deciding about?
- How to use elements?
- When to use elements?
- Why to use elements?

What are the elements or tools in your disposal?
- language
- words/vocabulary
- voice
- sound
- white space
- visual shape
- citation
- image
- figure of speech
