A walking religion.
A walking spirituality.
No real home or church or community?
- Or does the home, church, or community move with me?
- How can we maintain movement without totally being alone?
	- To be in a nomadic spiritual caravan with other who are also willing to move with us.
	- Or to be in a very lose community of travelers, traveling for two weeks and then meeting up at a tavern every two weeks or so.

Walks will still be important—my central metaphor in the jiyū shūkyō I'm creating.
