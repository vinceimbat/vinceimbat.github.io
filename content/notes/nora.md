At the well  
I heard a song.

It quenched my thirst  
Freed me from humidity  
With the coolness of its caress  
And resonance

Then I drifted with the wind  
To conquer the sweltering city.

The awakened masses  
Rose and confined their voices  
In a tight embrace  
To never release again.

They enthroned her  
On the stage so her wholeness  
Won't leave their gaze.

She, the end of all praises  
Today and many mornings  
Silent in front  
A cup of coffee  
With an unlighted cigarette  
Or none.

The white curtain behind her.  
She in everyone's hearts.
