When the world ended yesterday I was at my room
staring down at the rat's skull stuck
on the roof of our dirty kitchen
quietly waiting for its decomposition.
The vines remained hanging from the roof,
and caterpillars continued crawling through them,
weaving cocoons in nooks.

The world ended without me knowing
how exactly to clean this roof.
