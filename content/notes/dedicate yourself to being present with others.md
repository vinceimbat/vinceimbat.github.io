title: Dedicate yourself to being present with others

One way to [[focus on audience experience and personal fulfillment]] is to prioritize the opportunity to be present with your audience. Aspire to be someone who is there when they need to experience joy, when they need to learn, when they need a helping hand, and when they simply need to know that there is hope.
