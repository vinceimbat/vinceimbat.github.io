My mission is to [[living|live]] my life deliberately, informed by [[my jiyu shukyo|my jiyū shūkyō]]. Every day, I try to "suck all the marrow out of life." I maintain daily practices that help me do so. [[seiza|Seiza meditation]] and [[walking]] help me directly interact with the universe, while mindful [[reading]], [[journaling]], and [[my studying process|studying]] through my [[talahardin]] help me learn from existing archetypes of the life I want to live.

I compliment my personal practices with activities that lead me back to my *kapwa*, *lipunan*, and *sansinubkob*. I join and facilitate [[meditation|meditations]] and conversations with the communities I'm a part of. Mindful that my actions always affect others, I strive to always practice [[compassion]] and deepen my connection with others at every moment.

My [[jiyu shukyo|jiyū shūkyō]] is expressed as a liberative and socially conscious art practice centered around [[writing]]. My goal is to create artifacts that serve as spiritual footprints that may be of help in some way to fellow jiyūshūkyōjins.

To support myself in this mission, I take care of my [[mind]] and body, so that I'm always at my best state. Mindful that how I move through the material economy affects how I help build the world I want to live in, I try to provide for my material needs through meaningful [[work]], use resources efficiently, and limit my interaction with the exchange economy by developing [[self-reliance|self-reliance]].
