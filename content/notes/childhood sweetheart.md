Created: 9 July 2015

This is the story  
Of my romantic affair  
With a childhood sweetheart 

We were of the same age  
23  
1992 when we both were born

Her birth was not like any other birth  
She was born  
In a factory

Alas, we met in 1992  
The year when I first saw light  
And Mama Eral saw heaven

I grew up with her  
As she was bigger than me  
I rode at her back
