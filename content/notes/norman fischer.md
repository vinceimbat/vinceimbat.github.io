There are a lot of things to learn from Fischer, and his connection and engagement with [[language poetry]] makes him more attractive for my study.

I love what he said about making it intact as the whole point—if not a necessary task in life. We don't have a choice but to remain in tact. And if there is anything a religious life must do for me and the world, it must be that.

Fischer had clear reasons and motivations for pursuing [[experimental writing]]: Zen's connection with language philosophy and practice. Fischer's motivations triggered thoughts about [[my motivations for studying experimental writing]].

---

Fischer deliberately built a [[poetics]] based on his [[spirituality]]. This involves a commitment to [[zen|Zen]] and deepening into it. I must do the same with my own religious inclinations.
