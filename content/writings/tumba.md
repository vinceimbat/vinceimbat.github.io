This poem and its translation was published in *Walang Pahimakas: Anthology of Poems on Duterte’s “War on Drugs”* published by Amnesty International and Mountain Beacon in 2026.

---

Dugo itong  
humalo na sa pawis kong  
tumatawid sa noo  
nagbabadyang pumatak  
sa mainit na kalsadang  
pumipigil sa'king mukha.
  
Paalam, 'nay.  
Patawad.
  
Marahil,  
ito na ang hangganan  
ng lahat ng iyong  
paninimdim.
