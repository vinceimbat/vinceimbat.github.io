The following zine was published by Bangkô in July 2024 and was first sold at BLTX at Tomas Morato in August 2024. The zine, which I co-authored with Jesa Suganob, used [[walking]] as a method and platform for writing epistolary essays. The work was a finalist in the 2024 Big Other Reader's Choice Award.
